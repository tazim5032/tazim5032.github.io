JobFit privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

JobFit is a browser extension that compares your résumé with the job post you are viewing. It has no server and no account. This page explains what it stores and where your data goes.

What JobFit stores

Everything below is kept in your browser's extension storage, on your device only:

  • Your résumé text, the skills found in it and the file name.
  • AI provider keys you choose to add (Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter).
  • Your settings, such as letter length, tone and theme.
  • Saved jobs in the tracker: title, company, location, link, status, score, notes and dates.
  • Recent analysis results and cover letters, so they open instantly the second time.

JobFit does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

What leaves your device

Only when you add an AI key and use an AI feature (AI analysis, AI red-flag check or cover letter), JobFit sends the job post's text and your résumé text to the one AI provider you picked, using your key. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and nothing is sent at all if you use the offline score only.

That provider handles the request under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Groq privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy (Gemini API)
  • OpenRouter privacy policy

Your keys are sent only to the provider they belong to. They are never included in data exports.

Reading web pages

JobFit reads the page you are on only to find a job post: on supported job sites when the page loads, and on any other page when you open JobFit there or right-click selected text and choose "Check this text with JobFit". It does not read your browsing history.

Your control

  • Settings → Export all data downloads a copy of everything JobFit stores, without your keys.
  • Settings → Delete all data erases it. Removing the extension also erases it.
  • The tracker can export your saved jobs as CSV or JSON.

Selling and sharing

JobFit does not sell, rent or share your data, and does not use it for anything other than the features described above. The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: open an issue or message the developer through the contact details on the Chrome Web Store listing.
